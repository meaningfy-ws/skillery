# Services catalogue

Every Meaningfy engagement leads to the same place: **Semantic Layer adoption**, but no two
clients buy the same slice of it. A client's first paid step is
[Discovery & Onboarding](../engagement-lifecycle.md), whose Deep tier produces the **Decision
Package** (a recommendation, in/out scope, a pilot-to-scale roadmap, and a buy/build/defer call);
from there, a build contract scopes against **one** of the
building blocks below, **several** of them, or the **whole** Semantic Layer, always as a controlled,
priced piece of work (see
[`engagement-lifecycle.md#the-outcome-semantic-layer-adoption`](../engagement-lifecycle.md#the-outcome-semantic-layer-adoption)).
This page is the sellable catalogue of those blocks: the page a salesperson or a client-facing
consultant uses to describe what's actually on offer, one line per service, with what it includes,
what it produces, and who in the catalogue does the work.

## Reading this catalogue

Every service page below follows the same template, so the six are comparable at a glance: what it
is, the problem it solves for the client, what's included, what you actually get, which skill in the
Meaningfy catalogue does the work (or the honest admission that none does yet), and how it connects
back to the Semantic Layer as a whole.

Three of the six blocks (data mappings & integration, analytics/BI connections, and agentic
framework development) have **no dedicated skill in this catalogue today**. That's stated plainly on
each of their pages rather than papered over with a citation that doesn't hold up: Meaningfy can and
does discuss these needs with clients (see
[`semantic-consulting-coach`](../../../skills/semantic-consulting-coach/SKILL.md)'s service-family list),
but nothing here yet codifies how the work gets built. Training & capability building is in the same
position: the RACI matrix names the activity (`CBLC-lite`/`CBLC-standalone` in
[`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md)) but no skill owns it.

Prices and durations are **not invented here**. Where the repo already states one, this catalogue
cites it: the Discovery & Onboarding Light (1–2 day workshop) and Deep (4–6 weeks, calendar-boxed)
tiers. Everything downstream of Discovery & Onboarding is scoped per engagement, priced through the
[Decision Package](../../../skills/decision-package/SKILL.md) and the
[estimation](../../../skills/estimation/SKILL.md) discipline, not guessed at here.
