# Training & capability building

## What it is

Meaningfy can build a client's semantic model, systems, and agents. But the client's own team has to
be able to use, extend, and eventually maintain them without Meaningfy in the room. Training &
capability building is the work of transferring that ability: teaching the client's people the model,
the tooling, and the working practices well enough that they can keep the Semantic Layer alive and
growing on their own.

## The problem it solves

A build that only Meaningfy understands is a build the client can't safely change once the
engagement ends: every future update becomes another paid ticket back to Meaningfy, and the client
never actually owns what it paid for. This service exists so capability transfer is treated as a
deliverable in its own right, not an afterthought squeezed into the last week of a build contract.

## What's included

**No skill in this catalogue owns training and capability-building work.** The activity is named
twice in [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md)'s RACI matrix: as `CBLC-lite`
(Capacity-Building Life Cycle, run inside an existing software contract; accountable: Solution
Builder) and `CBLC-standalone` (a dedicated, stand-alone training engagement; accountable: Solution
Shipper). Neither row cites an owning skill, and a search of every skill under `skills/` for
training or capability-building content, including for "CBLC" itself, found nothing. The roles and
the accountability are defined; the method is not.

## What you get (deliverable)

Not yet standardised. Once a training/capability-building skill exists, this section states the
concrete artefact (e.g. a training curriculum, hands-on sessions, and a documented handover
checklist). Today, the deliverable is whatever is agreed case by case in the engagement's Decision
Package.

## Owning skill(s)

**No owning skill exists.** Confirmed twice over: `roles-and-raci.md` names the activity and its
accountable role but cites no skill, and a full search of `skills/` for training-related content
turns up nothing to cite instead.

## Where it fits

Training & capability building is one of the six building blocks the Semantic Layer decomposes into;
see
[`engagement-lifecycle.md#the-outcome-semantic-layer-adoption`](../engagement-lifecycle.md#the-outcome-semantic-layer-adoption).

work against.
