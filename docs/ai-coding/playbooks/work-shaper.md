# Work Shaper: playbook

> Role definition, mandate, and RACI cells: [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper).

Seeded from the Employee Handbook's *Project Owner: the Work Shaper* mandate, adapted to today's
way of working. You own and steer the `epic-planning` skill (and its `epic-planner` agent
counterpart), governing and checking rather than hand-writing every artifact. See
[`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper).

See [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper)'s Work Shaper block for this role's
position on the Architect–Builder spectrum (not restated here). It is also this repo's explicit
**bridge role**: it consults with the Solution Architect and coordinates with Sales/Presales at
its boundary, but the activity itself (turning a decided scope into a shaped, buildable bet) is
build-plane work, which is why this playbook lives here rather than in a separate cross-domain tier.

## Your stages

- **Epic shaping**: you are accountable (`A/R`) for the Work Breakdown Structure, the project
  roadmap, the Epic Pitch (Shape-Up), the risk list, and the ongoing mapping of discovered work into
  stories with the Builder. See
  [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper).
- **Shape the Epic / Derive the PLAN**: the steps inside the build loop where your
  artifacts (`proposal.md`, `design.md` + `tasks.md`) are authored. See
  [`build-lifecycle.md`](../build-lifecycle.md#epic-tier-one-epic-at-a-time--the-sdlc-loop).

The artifact you produce differs by plane (an Epic pitch for a software build, an ontology/model
project, or a training engagement), same role, different plane. See
[`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper).

## Skills you invoke

- [`epic-planning`](../../../skills/epic-planning/SKILL.md): the skill this role owns and steers;
  drives Epic/PLAN authoring.

Full list: [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper).

## Handed to you by

- **Sales/Presales** and the **Technical Consultant**, via the Decision Package's execution brief or
  direct-to-build's own elicitation, hand you the decided scope to shape into a bet. See
  [`../../ai-sales/engagement-lifecycle.md`](../../ai-sales/engagement-lifecycle.md#the-outcome-semantic-layer-adoption).
- The **Solution Architect** consults (`C`) during Epic shaping (documented collaboration, not
  a conflation). See
  [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#raci-matrix).

## You hand off to

- The shaped Epic (PLAN) hands off to the **Solution Builder** for the build loop, once the clarity
  gate is passed. See
  [`build-lifecycle.md`](../build-lifecycle.md#epic-tier-one-epic-at-a-time--the-sdlc-loop).
- You remain present through delivery to oversee the shape (is this still the bet made, is the
  appetite intact), but the delivery-close accountability itself belongs to the **Solution Shipper**.
  See [`roles-and-raci.md`](../../roles-and-raci.md#work-shaper).

## Your done

The Epic is shaped and ready to build when its PLAN (`design.md` + `tasks.md`) passes the clarity
gate at ≥9/10. See
[`dod-quality-gates.md`](../dod-quality-gates.md#the-build-tier-gate-set).
