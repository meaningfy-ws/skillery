PASS if the email is in a formal, contractual register: full sentences, neutral commitment verbs
(for example "will provide"), no contractions or casual asides; it summarises the offer (scope, price
excluding VAT, delivery time) without restating the quotation's line items or terms; and it ends with
a clear next step for the client.
FAIL if the email is conversational or salesy, imposes a pitch structure (problem, why it matters,
approach, value), invents terms the prompt did not give, or rewrites the quotation itself.
