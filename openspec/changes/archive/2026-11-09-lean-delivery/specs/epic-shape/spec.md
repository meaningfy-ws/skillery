## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Planning effort follows the appetite
A PLAN's depth SHALL follow its appetite. A TINY or SMALL EPIC SHALL run the clarity gate at most
twice; gaps left after the second run SHALL go to the developer as one question round, and a gap
the developer accepts SHALL be recorded as a `DEC-` in `design.md`, the one case in which a PLAN
proceeds below 9/10. Elicitation SHALL ask its questions in rounds (lean-delivery DEC-6, DEC-7).

#### Scenario: A SMALL PLAN at 8.6 after two runs
- **WHEN** a SMALL PLAN scores 8.6 on its second clarity-gate run
- **THEN** the remaining gaps go to the developer in one round, and no third revision runs
