## Goals it serves
Checking whether a plan or strategy paper is a strategy at all, and rewriting it into one: naming
the single critical obstacle, choosing an approach that rules things out, deriving a short set of
coordinated, staffed actions with a stop list, and setting near targets under ambiguity. It also
helps read a market shift, a rival's inertia or one's own drift. It fits Meaningfy's own business
strategy, an annual or portfolio plan, and a client's strategy paper read during consulting. It is
not for message structure (`executive-communication`), not for coaching the founder's thinking on
Meaningfy's business (`semantic-consulting-coach`), not for positioning or pricing, and never a
source of a price (DEC-19).
