## Goals it serves
Building a whole marketing plan on one page: choosing a niche, stating why a buyer should pick the
company, choosing one channel to start with, designing a lead magnet and the mails that follow,
measuring acquisition cost against lifetime value, designing the experience after the sale and a
referral or partner programme. For Meaningfy it fits planning company marketing and reviewing a
channel or a referral idea; marketing has no house rule yet (DEC-10). Not for pricing, guarantees or
discounts (`estimation`, `semantic-consulting-coach`), a proposal (`proposal-writing`), a sales call
or follow-up (`sales-conversations`), qualifying or scoring a deal (`sales-qualification`), or the
CRM, which is Meaningfy's Odoo. Its references on conversion, lifetime value and referrals are where
it meets Meaningfy's sales rules head on.
