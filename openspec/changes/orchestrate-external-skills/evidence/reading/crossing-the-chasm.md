## Goals it serves
Taking a technology product from its first enthusiastic buyers to mainstream, risk-averse buyers:
choosing one narrow target segment, listing what that segment needs beyond the product itself,
writing a positioning statement against the alternative they use today, shifting the message from
vision to proof, deciding the channel, and choosing the next segments once the first is won. A
person would use it for a go-to-market plan, a beachhead choice, or a diagnosis of why growth stopped
after the early adopters. It is written for product companies, mostly SaaS; for Meaningfy it fits a
productised offering or a client's product, and only informs a positioning decision, which stays the
owner's (`semantic-consulting-coach`, `references/semantic-consulting-domain.md`, lines 24-27). Not
for pricing a deal, qualifying a lead or writing outreach: those stay with the sales skills.
