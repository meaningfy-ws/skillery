## Goals it serves
Deciding how an offering is positioned: what buyers compare it with (including doing nothing), what
only it has, what that is worth to them, who cares most, and which market frame makes all of that
obvious at once. It fits a new offering, a crowded market, a repositioning, or the symptom that
prospects do not grasp what the offering does, and it gives a workshop to bring a team to one
description. It is not for scoring a deal or qualifying a lead (`sales-qualification`), for setting
a price (`estimation` and the offering document), or for writing the final message (that is
`executive-communication` with `meaningfy-voice`). For Meaningfy's own consulting positioning,
`semantic-consulting-coach` leads the thinking and this skill can supply the canvas.
