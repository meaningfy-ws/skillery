## Goals it serves
Understanding why a client buys, stays or leaves: writing the job a client is trying to get done
without naming a solution, mapping what pushes them to change and what holds them back, seeing every
alternative they could use (including doing nothing), and interviewing recent buyers or lost clients
along the timeline of their decision. For Meaningfy it fits the design of a service or offering
(what job a Proof of Value is hired for), the *why us against the alternative* line and the
objection drivers in a dossier, and a lost-deal or churned-client review. It is written for product
companies; its pricing, guarantee and copy advice assumes a product launch. Not for setting a price
(`estimation`), not for scoring a deal (`sales-qualification`), and not for writing the outreach text
(`meaningfy-voice`, `sales-conversations`).
