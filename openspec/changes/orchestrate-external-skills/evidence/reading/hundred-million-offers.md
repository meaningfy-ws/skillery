## Goals it serves
Shaping what is offered so that it is easy to accept: stating the client's outcome in their words,
raising their belief that it will work, shortening the time to a first result and cutting the work
it asks of them; listing what stands between the client and that result and answering each obstacle;
naming the offer so the right buyer recognises it. Under Meaningfy's overrides it helps design an
offering and strengthen the value-equation line of a dossier (DEC-19 lets it inform the design). It
is not for setting a price, a discount, a payment plan or a guarantee (DEC-7, DEC-19): those come
from the offering document, `estimation` and the owner. It is not used in a tender, and its hype
register is not used with compliance, CFO or bank buyers.
