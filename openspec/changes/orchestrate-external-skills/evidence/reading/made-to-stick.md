## Goals it serves
Making a message understood and remembered: cutting an idea to its one core, replacing abstract
words with things a reader can picture, making a claim believable, giving it a person and a story,
and spotting where expert knowledge hides the point from an outsider. A person would reach for it to
sharpen a tagline, a value proposition, a pitch or talk, a landing page, a demo opening or an
all-hands message, or to ask why a message is not landing. It suits the teach register and
marketing copy. It is not for binding text (quotation, statement of work, tender answer), where
Meaningfy forbids persuasion, and it does not decide the structure of a decision document, which is
`executive-communication`'s; in a sales email it gives way to the outreach rules of
`sales-conversations` and the voice's outreach channel.
