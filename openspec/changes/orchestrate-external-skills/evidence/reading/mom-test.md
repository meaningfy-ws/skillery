## Goals it serves
Finding out whether a problem is real before building or selling a solution to it: planning a
customer-discovery talk, wording questions about what the person already did rather than what they
might do, turning praise and feature wishes back into facts, ending each talk with a concrete next
step, and pooling the notes so the team changes its mind on evidence. For Meaningfy it helps when
testing a new service idea or a new market with people who are not yet prospects, and when the owner
wants the questions in a first meeting to be about the client's past attempts. It is written for
product founders; prices, discounts and deadlines in its examples are product launch moves. Not for
a live deal: the deal's questions come from the MEDDPICC bank of `sales-qualification`, the meeting
plan and follow-up from `sales-conversations`.
