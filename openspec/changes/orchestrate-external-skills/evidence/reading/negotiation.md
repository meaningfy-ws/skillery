## Goals it serves
Preparing for a conversation where the other side holds back or pushes: listening so that they talk,
naming their worry before it derails the meeting, asking open questions that let them solve our
problem, finding the hidden constraint (a capped budget, a boss who decides), and knowing one's
walk-away point before the meeting. It suits a hard client call, a partner dispute, a supplier or
salary negotiation, and a stalled deal. It is not for setting or moving a Meaningfy price (DEC-19):
price comes from `estimation` and the offering, and its bargaining and anchoring sections are not used
on the sell side. It is not for written tender clarifications, and not a replacement for the selling
method in `sales-conversations`, which already adopts its listening tools with Meaningfy's limits.
