## Goals it serves
Making a page, a product flow or a campaign more convincing: a landing page that earns trust, a
testimonial or logo section, an onboarding or referral flow, an urgency message, a warmer support
or error text. It also gives a usable audit of persuasive material for manipulation: the
transparency test, the truth, benefit and freedom check, and the vulnerable-user safeguards
(`references/ethics.md`, lines 20-77, 126-146). Most of its examples are consumer and SaaS
marketing. It is the wrong tool for a tender, a quotation, an offer or a statement of work (binding
text, where Meaningfy uses no persuasion), for anything that sets a price, a discount or a
guarantee, and for one-to-one sales drafts, which follow the selling method of
`sales-conversations`. Its own description sends deal tactics to `negotiation`.
