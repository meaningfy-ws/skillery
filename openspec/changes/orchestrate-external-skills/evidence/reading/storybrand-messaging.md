## Goals it serves
Making a company's message clear: a one-sentence statement of what the company does, a homepage or
landing page laid out as a story with the customer at the centre, a consistent message across
LinkedIn, talks and press, nurture mails for a subscriber list, and a check of existing copy against
the seven elements. For Meaningfy it fits website and LinkedIn copy, the one-liner for the company or
for one offering, and a speaker introduction. Marketing has no house rule yet (DEC-10), so the
framework is used as it is there. Not for a sales meeting, a follow-up or an objection
(`sales-conversations`), a proposal (`proposal-writing`), a price (`estimation`), or anything in a
tender; its `references/sales-conversations.md` is the part most at odds with Meaningfy's rules.
