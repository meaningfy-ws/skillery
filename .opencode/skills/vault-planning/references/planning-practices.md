# Planning practices behind vault-planning

Three established practices, combined so each does the job it is best at. None is followed to the
letter; this file says what is taken from each, and what is left.

## Multi-scale planning: the cascade

Plan at several time scales at once, each setting the frame for the next: a long horizon states
direction, a shorter one chooses what to push now, the shortest decides today. The benefit is that a
day's choices can be traced to something the owner cares about, and a day that serves nothing is
visible as "unplanned".

**Taken:** four horizons in the plans file (year, quarter, month, week) plus the day in the daily
note; every item cites the item above it.
**Left:** detailed time-blocking of the day; the daily note lists plans, it does not schedule hours.

## OKRs at the quarter

An objective says where to go, in words; a few key results say how you will know you got there,
each measurable by someone else (a number, a date, a yes/no). The quarter is short enough to adjust
and long enough for real outcomes.

**Taken:** objectives with measurable key results at the quarter; objectives alone at the year and the
month.
**Left:** scoring rituals (0.0 to 1.0 grades) and stretch-goal targets; a review states wins and misses
in words.

## The GTD weekly review

Once a week, get clear (empty the inboxes), get current (look at every active project and waiting
item), and get creative (decide next week's focus). It is what keeps any system trusted.

**Taken:** the weekly sweep of `00 Inbox/`, staging, projects without a session, and stale to-dos; the
"next focus" section.
**Left:** GTD's contexts and next-action lists; the vault's `tasks.md` and `To-Do.md` already hold
actions.

## Reviews at each horizon

Every period ends with a short record: wins, misses (with a line of why), what carries over, next
focus. Longer reviews read the shorter reviews, not the raw days: a quarterly review reads three
monthly reviews, a yearly review four quarterly ones. Plans start from the latest review of their
horizon, so each period learns from the last.

## Cadence

| Horizon | Plan | Review |
|---|---|---|
| week | Monday, or the last working day before | Friday, or Monday before planning |
| month | first working day | last working day |
| quarter | first week of the quarter | last week of the quarter |
| year | first weeks of January | December |

The cadence is a default; the owner's calendar wins.
