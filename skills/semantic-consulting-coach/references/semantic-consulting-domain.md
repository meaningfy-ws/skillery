# Semantic Consulting Domain Map

Grounds coaching questions in Meaningfy's real offering, business models and markets. Not for
lecturing the user (they know the domain) or pushing a solution: flag a service or model as a
*hypothesis* until the user confirms it applies.

## 0. Current positioning & vocabulary (meaningfy.ws)

Mirror the client's and the firm's own words; do not impose generic consulting language.

**Mission.** "We empower organisations to build knowledge-centric systems where information is
clear, machines understand context, and people make better decisions based on shared meaning."

**Live positioning.** A *semantic & datacentric partner* delivering *interoperability solutions
for EU organisations* (public and private). Named pains: data harmonisation, data silos, turning
raw data into actionable insight. House phrases: "borrow our brain", "digital brain", "single
source of truth", "semantic consensus", "semantic enrichment".

**Emerging positioning (likely repositioning).** Toward *trusted enterprise AI*: "the semantic
layer between your enterprise data and your AI"; "We structure your data, AI extracts the value";
"Trusted Data. Trusted Reporting. Trusted AI."; AI that is "deterministic, not probabilistic"
through knowledge-graph grounding; reporting that is "audit-ready, board-ready, regulator-ready".

The shift from interoperability-for-the-public-sector to semantic-layer-for-trusted-AI is a **live
positioning decision** (which market, which message, which buyer). The AI-grounding story is the
sharpest new value story and the one most likely to commoditise if pitched as "just RAG": probe
which framing wins which deal, and what stays uniquely Meaningfy's.

## 1. Service families (offering layer)

The **Proof of Value** heads "Strategic data & semantics advisory" and gates everything downstream.
The **decide vs build** axis matters most: advisory helps the client decide, engineering helps them
build, and blurring them silently is where margin and IP leak.

| Family | Includes | Decide / Build | IP exposure | Natural stopping point / handover |
|--------|----------|----------------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Strategic data & semantics advisory** | Data & semantic strategy, semantic-layer strategy, governance operating-model design, roadmaps, maturity assessment | Decide | High (method = the product) | Endorsed roadmap + decision |
| **Data governance & management** | Governance framework, MDM, metadata, data quality, lineage, cataloguing, stewardship model | Decide → Build | Medium–High (operating model is IP) | Operating model live + stewards trained |
| **Semantic engineering** | Ontology engineering, taxonomy management, knowledge graph creation, data mapping, vocabulary/standards alignment | Build | High (modelling patterns) | Validated model + tests + handover docs |
| **Semantic interoperability** | Cross-system mapping, standards conformance, shared vocabularies, exchange schemas | Build | Medium | Conformant exchange in production |
| **NLP & AI grounding** | Semantic search, extraction, NER, classification, annotation; RAG / LLM grounding via the semantic layer | Build | Medium (pipelines & prompts) | Extraction or grounded AI in production |
| **Semantic-layer enablement** | Standing up the semantic layer, capability transfer, training, accelerators | Build → Transfer | Medium (accelerators scale) | Client team self-sufficient |
| **Applied research** | Horizon Europe (RIA/IA/CSA), FNR INTER (Luxembourg); TRL 1–3 prototyping; consortia (EU Publications Office, infeurope, Univ. Luxembourg/Bologna) | Explore | Low–Medium (often publishable) | Deliverable / publication / prototype |
| **Product development** | Reusable products & accelerators: **Mapping Workbench** (proprietary), **RDF Differ** | Productise | IP becomes the asset | Released, licensable product |
| **Bespoke software** | Custom apps, PoC/prototype, pilot/MVP on data-centric foundations | Build | Low–Medium | Working software + support |
| **Tool resale** | Third-party triple stores, catalogues, MDM, taxonomy tools | Transact | Low | Licence sold (+ optional implementation) |

Locate an engagement in this table, then probe the decide/build blur, the IP exposure and whether
a stopping point is defined. The public site groups the same work as *IT Business Consulting*,
*Enterprise Knowledge Graph*, *Semantic Data Strategy & Architecture*, *NLP Technologies
Deployment*, *Software Development* and *Research & Development*; standards in play: SEMIC Style
Guide, TOGAF/ArchiMate.

**Two engagement models; reconcile them.** The public site shows a four-step funnel (*Discovery →
Proposal → Implementation → Delivery*); the internal model is the free→paid structure of
[`engagement-lifecycle.md`](engagement-lifecycle.md). A recurring coaching task is aligning the
public story with the internal boundaries, so the paid tiers are not lost inside a "Discovery" step
that reads as free.

## 2. Business / revenue models (meta-layer)

The mix is the question: day-rate advisory, fixed-scope delivery, grant-funded research, product
licensing, tool resale, partnering and subcontracting. Watch for the mix turning Meaningfy into a
labour business (day rates), losing margin to scope creep (fixed scope), research that distracts
from revenue, resale that anchors it as a reseller rather than an advisor, and subcontracting that
makes it invisible capacity behind a prime.

## 3. Markets: B2B vs B2G

B2G centres on EU institutions and programmes (EU Publications Office, Horizon Europe,
SEMIC-aligned interoperability): tenders and framework agreements, consortia or subco often
required, outputs that may be public. There the free-PoC-before-tender trap and IP exposure to
rival bidders are acute, and discovery must fit procurement rules. B2B references include large
enterprises and advisories (KPMG, PwC, NTT Data, Cargolux), where the trusted-reporting story
lands; the lever is negotiating paid, bounded discovery and protecting the price anchor.

## 4. Where IP and scope risk concentrate

Highest exposure (protect; demonstrate with reference patterns, not live method): the ontology
engineering method and modelling patterns; the data-mapping approach and mapping patterns; the
governance / MDM operating model. Lower exposure (safer to demo or tease): tool capabilities and
platform features; published research; generic maturity assessments and high-level roadmaps.

Check every "should we do this for free / cheap / fast?" against which of these it exposes, and
whether the engagement blurs decide and build.
