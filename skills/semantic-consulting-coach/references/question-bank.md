# Question Bank

High-leverage questions the **coach asks the user** (the consultant), by layer and
semantic-service area. Use them to elicit thinking, never to lead to a predetermined answer; one
sharp question beats three soft ones. Questions the user asks prospects are a different bank
([`presales-discovery.md`](presales-discovery.md)). In Exploration, open with "What problem are you
really trying to solve here, for *your* business, not the client?"; in Alignment, "What is the one
thing that must be true?".

## By layer

- **Business meta-layer:** "Where does your business make money because of judgement, not labour?"
  · "Which clients increase your clarity, and which dilute it?" · "What are you implicitly
  subsidising today?" · "What should *never* be customised, and what must remain scarce?"
- **Service layer:** "Which services help clients *decide*, and which help them *build*?" · "Where
  do you blur advisory and delivery unintentionally?" · "Which services protect your intellectual
  capital, and which expose it?" · "Where are the explicit stopping points and handovers?"
- **Client orchestration layer:** "What must the client understand before they can buy wisely?" ·
  "Where do you over-educate too early, giving away strategy for free?" · "How do you decide a
  client is ready for execution?" · "Is discovery here a paid, bounded value activity, or unpaid
  scope creep?"

## By semantic-service area

- **Advisory vs engineering:** "Is this engagement helping the client *decide* or *build*, and are
  you pricing it as what it is?" · "Where does your advisory quietly turn into unpaid engineering?"
- **Ontology, taxonomy, knowledge graph:** "Does this expose your modelling method, or just its
  output?" · "What's reusable as a pattern across clients, and what must stay bespoke?"
- **Governance, MDM, metadata, quality, lineage, cataloguing:** "Are you selling a governance
  *operating model* (scarce IP) or governance *labour*?" · "Where does the handover to the client's
  stewards actually happen?"
- **Research, product, resale:** "Is the research funding R&D and credibility, or distracting from
  revenue?" · "Does reselling this tool position you as an advisor, or as a reseller?" · "What
  would it take for this accelerator to become a licensable product?"
- **Partnering and subcontracting:** "As a sub, are you trading margin for deal flow, and is your
  brand visible?" · "What makes you the prime here rather than someone else's capacity?"
- **B2B vs B2G:** "Does this discovery fit the procurement rules, or are you working for free inside
  a tender?" · "Who in the consortium owns the client relationship after delivery?"
- **Free → paid boundary:** "Is this client in orientation or decision, and are you charging
  accordingly?" · "Which of these questions are you about to answer for free that belong in a paid
  Proof of Value?" · "What would the Decision Package need to contain for them to commit safely?" ·
  "Where are you explaining to persuade, when you could be proposing the Proof of Value?"

## Negotiation and communication

Interests and leverage before tactics. "Have you fixed your walk-away *before* the room, not in
it?" · "Which side can walk away more easily?" · "What concession pattern are you signalling, and
what does it train them to expect next time?" · "Are you optimising the deal, the relationship, or
the precedent you set?" Before drafting: "What objection will they raise first?" · "Is this the
right channel and moment, or are you polishing the message and ignoring the timing?"

## Meta-cognitive prompts (use sparingly)

For a user conflating roles or optimising the wrong thing: "Are you thinking as a founder, a
consultant or a delivery lead right now?" · "Is this uncertainty something to resolve, or something
to preserve?" · "If you had to explain this to a senior peer, what would you simplify?"
