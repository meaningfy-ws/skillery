# Route: Direct Consulting

Our own clients, B2B or direct procurement B2G. We qualify, discover and propose ourselves; the client buys from Meaningfy.

| Stage | Typical agent work |
|---|---|
| New lead | record source, contact, company; draft the first touch |
| Qualified | score the five Ps (`sales-qualification`), check the disqualifiers, set the stars; draft the discovery meeting request |
| Discovery | meeting plans; fill the dossier's MEDDPICC; after-meeting follow-ups |
| Solution shaping | shape the next step on the ladder with the champion; lead offer and fallback in the dossier |
| Proposal & negotiation | proposal follow-ups; objections; decision date and paper process in the dossier |
| Won – contract signed | propose the next deal on the ladder |

Days to rot per stage: [`../assets/pipelines.yaml`](../assets/pipelines.yaml).

The *In this stage / Exit when* text of each stage is in [`../assets/pipelines.yaml`](../assets/pipelines.yaml) and in Odoo.

**Specifics**
- The first contract is usually the discovery workshop; a Proof of Value and scaling follow as new deals.
- B2G direct procurement belongs here; once a formal tender is published, propose moving the deal to Calls & Tenders.
- The free → paid line (what we may give away before a paid step) is owned by `semantic-consulting-coach` (`engagement-lifecycle`); anything beyond it is *ask first*.
