# Route: Calls & Tenders

Public and private tenders and grant calls (R&D or other), usually with consortium partners. Deadline-driven: the procurement rules or the funder set the pace, not the buyer's conversation. Tags: *Public Procurement Tender*, *Private Tender*, *R&D Grants*. Our role goes in the deal name or the dossier: prime, consortium member, subcontractor, coordinator.

| Stage | Typical agent work |
|---|---|
| Watch | Get the documents, deadline, lots or topics; summarise fit in the dossier |
| Go / no-go | Write a go/no-go scorecard (fit, chance to win, effort, partners needed, eligibility of our role) for the owner's decision within 3 days; the score sets the stars |
| Consortium & strategy | Partner shortlist and outreach drafts; win themes mapped to the scoring grid; work and budget split |
| Writing | Deadline countdown in every run; checklist of the call's requirements, criterion by criterion |
| Submitted, awaiting result | Clarification answers; prepare the start if likely |
| Won – awarded & signed | Framework contracts continue in FWC Execution |

Days to rot per stage: [`../assets/pipelines.yaml`](../assets/pipelines.yaml).

## Tender etiquette (applies from publication to award)

- **Contacting the contracting authority.** Only through the call's official clarification channel, in writing. Never draft outreach to the authority's staff about the call. Meetings, market consultations and information days held before publication are fair game.
- **Writing the bid.** Write to the scoring grid: one section per award criterion, with evidence for each.
- **Before a consortium or subcontracting draft.** Check the call's rules on taking part in several bids, on subcontracting shares and on conflicts of interest. If unclear, ask the owner.
- **Persuasion.** No sales techniques: no scarcity, urgency, bonuses or guarantees. The value proof lives inside the call's rules. Hormozi and Voss tactics are not used here (see `sales-conversations` (selling method)).

## Go/no-go score

Each criterion 0–2 (total 0–8), in the dossier's *Attention score*, one line each with its source; the stars follow (`SKILL.md`, *Stars*: 6–8 ★★★, 4–5 ★★, 0–3 ★). Eligibility of our role is a gate, not a score: if we are not eligible, it is a no-go.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fit** with our services and references | weak | partial | strong, with a reference per main criterion |
| **Chance to win** | low (incumbent, price-driven) | open | good (known buyer, criteria that favour quality) |
| **Effort** (inverted: lower effort scores higher) | heavy for the value | moderate | light for the value |
| **Partners needed** | missing and hard to find | identified, not confirmed | none needed, or confirmed |

A no-go is proposed as lost (*ask first*), not starred.

## Other specifics

- The submission deadline is an activity of type *Deadline*, due on that date; every run checks deadlines in the next 14 days first. The result date, once the authority states it, is the deal's expected closing date (stage *Submitted, awaiting result*).
- The tender-watch mechanism lives elsewhere; this route starts when a call reaches Odoo.
- MEDDPICC maps onto the call documents: the paper process is the call, the economic buyer is the authority or funder, and the competition is the other bidders.
