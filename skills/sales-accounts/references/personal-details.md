# Personal details

Meaningfy treats the people it works with as people, not records. Remembering that someone's daughter just started school, that they love the Läckerli from Basel, or that they are training for a half-marathon is how a kind, attentive person behaves, and it is welcome when it comes from attention rather than surveillance. These rules keep it on the right side of that line, and of GDPR.

## Three rules for personal details

1. **Record generously what the person shared with us.** Anything they told the owner or a colleague, in a meeting, a call, a mail or a dinner: family and close relations, children and their ages, birthday, occasions, where they were born, grew up or lived, places and travels they love, hobbies, food, sports, likes and dislikes. Each entry carries the date and where it was said ("YYYY-MM-DD, at the conference booth, to the owner").
2. **Record what they published themselves**, under their own name and in public: a talk, an interview, a blog post, a public profile they wrote. Each entry carries the link and its date.
3. **Never research private life.** No searching for relatives, home address, private social-media accounts, genealogy, or anything the person did not share or publish. Never infer a personal detail ("probably has children"). Never record health, religion, political views, sexual orientation, ethnicity, union membership or criminal matters, even when the person mentioned them; if they matter for a meeting (a dietary need, an accessibility need), the owner notes it themselves.

## Where it lives

In the person profile's *Personal touches* section, on the contact's *Notes* (`comment`). Never in a deal, a mail draft's quoted text, a note visible to others, or the vault's run notes beyond a pointer ("personal touch added to contact 1234").

## How it is used

`sales-conversations` (selling method, *Personal touches*) says how: rarely, at most one per message, only when it is natural, and only from something the person shared with us.

## Forgetting

Every entry is dated and sourced, so a request to forget a person is complete: the owner removes the profile's personal section, or the whole contact, in Odoo. The agent never deletes; it lists what to remove when asked.
