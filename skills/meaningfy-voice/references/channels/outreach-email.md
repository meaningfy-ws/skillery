# Outreach email channel profile

How the company voice sounds in a commercial email to a prospect, client or partner: first
outreach, follow-up, reply, revival. Everything in `../company-voice.md` applies; this profile adds
only what the channel changes. What to send and when (patterns, cadence, the cc rule, prices) is
sales method, owned by `sales-conversations`.

## Tone

- **Warm, direct, specific, short.** Readable on a phone in 20 seconds.
- **One clear ask per message.** If there are two, the second waits for the next mail.
- **Match the thread.** Write in the thread's language and formality (vous, Sie,
  dumneavoastră), and never be more familiar than the last message received.
- **Every message adds something:** an insight, a sourced observation about them, a useful
  pointer. Never "just checking in" or "any update?".
- **Own our delays plainly**, in one line: "I owe you the offer since July; here it is."

## Never

- Fake deadlines or fake scarcity.
- Inflated claims or promises we cannot measure.
- Corporate openers ("I hope this email finds you well").
- Persuasion techniques in public procurement: there, evidence per criterion and plain
  compliance only.

## Sign-off

The sender's standard signature (name, title, phone, meeting link), as in their recent sent mail.

## Example

> Dear Ms Weber,
>
> At the Luxembourg data summit you said your team re-keys the same supplier codes into three
> systems. We fixed exactly that for a public registry last year by mapping the codes once to a
> shared vocabulary.
>
> Would a 20-minute call next week be useful to see whether it fits your case?
