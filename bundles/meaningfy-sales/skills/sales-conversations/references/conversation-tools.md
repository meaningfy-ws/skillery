# Conversation tools

The tools a meeting plan picks from. The principles, and when each source does not apply, are in [selling-method](selling-method.md). The after-meeting note says which tools were used.

## Discovery flow (adapted from Hormozi's C-L-O-S-E-R)

**Clarify** why they are here, in their words → **label** their problem → **overview** past attempts and what each missed, on the three pillars of the dossier's MEDDPICC (`sales-qualification`): a shared model, repeatable mappings, governance → **picture the outcome** for them, not our method → **address concerns** with proof and cover → **agree the next step**, who must be involved, and a date.

The flow does not close in the room unless the decision can be taken there. Do not use it when the partner leads the meeting.

## Commercial teaching (Challenger)

For a first meeting with a new prospect, before discovery goes deep, when the dossier shows no clear pain yet: the Challenger teaching sequence (warmer, reframe, rational drowning, emotional impact, a new way, our solution), built on one insight from [insights](insights.md). Meaningfy's conditions:
- the cost uses their own numbers or sourced ones; otherwise ask them for theirs;
- the story of an organisation like theirs is anonymised unless we have consent;
- Meaningfy is not named before the last step, which ends on the small first step on the ladder.

Then switch to listening for their reaction. Do not use it in tenders or when the partner leads the meeting.

## Listening (Voss)

Mirror, label, calibrated and no-oriented questions; aim for "that's right" before talking about the solution. Meaningfy's lines:
- **Accusation audit** only when the dossier shows evidence that the fear exists, such as a voiced objection or a loss note. Otherwise give the reassurance (references, insurance, backup) without naming the fear.
- **A graceful no**: "How am I supposed to do that?"
- **The focus question**: "What would have to be true for this to be an easy yes for you internally?"
- **People behind the table**: who can kill the deal, and who decides.

## Belief-breaking (Hormozi)

Their belief → why it doesn't hold → what is true instead → why it is true → the proof.
